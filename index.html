The Silk Road Evolutionary Split of the Genus Malus

Originating in the Tian Shan mountains of Central Asia, wild forest apples (Malus sieversii) traveled both west and east along ancient trade routes, hybridizing with distinct wild crabapples at opposite ends of Eurasia—before American cultivars reunited the two lineages in the 1870s.
